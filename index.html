About

Anton Orell Wiehe

Co-Founder of Pharos Labs and AdaLab. Teaching real robots to learn, and shipping AI that people actually use.

Hamburg, Germany

AI researcher and entrepreneur working on adaptive intelligence — lately with a robot arm on my desk. In a small home lab, I work on learning manipulation from human demonstrations with a low-cost SO-101 arm, using imitation policies like ACT and SmolVLA on the LeRobot stack. I also experiment with online reinforcement learning using learned reward models and human-in-the-loop feedback, all on a single GPU. I write up both the technical setup and the seven-month lab diary, dead ends included.

The other half of my work is taking models the last mile. At Pharos Labs I lead Regulaido, where AI-generated regulatory documents have to survive real scrutiny — every claim traceable to a source, deployed into enterprise environments alongside the writers who use it. Before that I co-founded AdaLab, where our critical care prediction work was published in BRAIN and our generative music video product Plazmapunk reached 100,000 creators.

The thread through all of it is one question: what makes a system adapt? I am drawn to mathematical formulations of curiosity, agents that set their own goals, and architectures that learn from their environment rather than just processing it — and to the much less glamorous work of making those ideas run reliably on real hardware, in front of real users.
